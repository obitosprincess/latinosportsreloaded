The Comeback Yankees Are For Real — Bronx Bombers Erase Four-Run Deficit, Improve to 7-1

Saturday's 9-7 win extended the Yankees' best opening stretch in over a decade. Two come-from-behind victories in the first eight games reveal a resilient identity forming early.

RM
Rich Mancuso
Senior Writer · Latino Sports since 2003
|May 9, 2026|4 min read
Photo: Bill Menzel / Latino Sports — Yankee Stadium, Bronx, NY

The Yankees' 9-7 win and 7-1 start to the 2026 season matches their best in franchise history. Saturday night ended with the Yankees' second comeback in their first eight games, and largest come-from-behind win since July 19 of last year at Atlanta.

After trailing 4-0 in the fourth, the Bronx Bombers scored two runs in the fifth, three in the sixth, a run in the seventh, and three more in the eighth. The bullpen held; the bats refused to quit.

An Identity Forming Early

"When we got down four, nobody changed in that dugout. That's the part you can't teach."— Aaron Boone, postgame
